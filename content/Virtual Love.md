#### **2. Explaining the Graph (45s)**
**[Action: Click Next to Slide 2 - Data Commentary]**

- **The Side (Y-axis):** This is the **Romantic Feeling** score. Higher means more 'love.'

#### **3. Finding 1: The "Looks" Floor (1m)**
**[Action: Click Next to Slide 3 - Finding 1: Orange Bars]**
"Now, look at the **Orange bars** (the average-looking group). 

Notice that whether the AI is 'Smart' (left) or 'Robotic' (right), the bars are almost the same height—and they are both very low. 

**The Finding:** If the AI's attractiveness is low, it doesn't matter how 'smart' or 'charming' the conversation is. The romantic feeling stays at the bottom. In short: Conversation alone cannot fix a lack of physical attraction."

---

#### **4. Finding 2: The "Intelligence" Multiplier (1m)**
**[Action: Click Next to Slide 4 - Finding 2: Blue Bars]**
"Now, let’s look at the **Blue bars** (the good-looking group). 

Something interesting happens here! When the AI is handsome, the 'Smart' version (left blue bar) is much higher than the 'Robotic' version (right blue bar). 

**The Finding:** Intelligence and personality **only** boost the romantic feeling when the character is already attractive. If he's handsome AND smart, the love feeling explodes!"
