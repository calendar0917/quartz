## 1. 等容过程 (Isochoric)
- 特征：$V = C, dV = 0$
- 功：$A = 0$
- 热量：$Q = \Delta E = \nu C_V \Delta T$
- 摩尔定体热容：$C_V = \frac{i}{2}R$

## 2. 等压过程 (Isobaric)
- 特征：$P = C$
- 功：$A = P(V_2 - V_1) = \nu R \Delta T$
- 热量：$Q = \nu C_P \Delta T$
- 摩尔定压热容：$C_P = C_V + R = \frac{i+2}{2}R$

## 3. 等温过程 (Isothermal)
- 特征：$T = C, \Delta E = 0$
- 热量与功：$Q = A = \nu RT \ln(\frac{V_2}{V_1}) = \nu RT \ln(\frac{P_1}{P_2})$

## 4. 绝热过程 (Adiabatic)
- 特征：$Q = 0$
- 过程方程：$PV^\gamma = C$, $TV^{\gamma-1} = C$, $P^{\gamma-1}T^{-\gamma} = C$
- 绝热指数（比热比）：$\gamma = C_P / C_V = (i+2)/i$
- 功：$A = -\Delta E = -\nu C_V (T_2 - T_1)$

## 5. 多方过程 (Polytropic)
- $PV^n = C$ ($n$ 为多方指数)
- 摩尔热容：$C = C_V - \frac{R}{n-1}$
